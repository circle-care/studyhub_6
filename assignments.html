💡 TMA tips
📖
Read the question carefully
Read it at least twice before starting. Identify exactly what is being asked.
✍️
Show all your working
Marks are awarded for method — even a wrong final answer can score marks if the working is clear.
✅
Always check your answer
Substitute back in, differentiate your integral, or try a test value to verify.
⏰
Submit at least a day early
Technical issues happen. Never submit in the final hour before the cut-off.
